How do I get a live link? (one-time setup, ~1 minute)
  1. Open the meal-plan .xlsx (delivered in chat) in Google Drive on your phone or computer, then tap Open with → Google Sheets (this makes an editable live copy).
  2. In Google Sheets, tap Share (top right) → under "General access" choose Anyone with the link → role Viewer → Done. This lets this page read it without logging in — it stays read-only to anyone else.
  3. Tap Copy link, come back here, paste it above, and tap Connect.
  4. Bookmark this page (or Add to Home Screen) afterward — the link is saved in the page's URL, so it reconnects automatically next time.
  5. From now on: edit ingredients, macros, or add whole new meal blocks directly in that Google Sheet from your phone, then hit ↻ Refresh here.
